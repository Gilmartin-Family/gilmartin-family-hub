Gilmartin Family Hub — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

About this app

Gilmartin Family Hub is a private family dashboard for viewing family calendar events and managing household chores, stars and rewards. It is intended for use by members of the Gilmartin household, not as a public service.

Information we access

When a family member authorizes Google Calendar access, the dashboard requests permission to read calendars available to the signed-in Google account, including event information such as titles, dates, times, locations and descriptions. The dashboard uses this information to display the family schedule. It does not need permission to edit Google Calendar events.

When a family member signs in using Google, Firebase Authentication processes account information needed to identify the signed-in user, such as their Google account identifier and email address.

Information we store

The dashboard stores household information such as chore definitions, weekly assignments, completion and excusal records, bonus stars, monthly targets, reward descriptions and reward history in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore. Some dashboard settings and temporary data may also be stored in the device's browser.

Calendar information is retrieved from Google Calendar to display events. The dashboard is not intended to create a public calendar or publish family event details.

How information is used and shared

Information is used only to operate the family dashboard and synchronize it between authorized family devices. We do not sell personal information or use Google Calendar data for advertising. The app relies on Google services, including Google Calendar, Google Authentication and Firebase, to provide these features. Information is processed by those services according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

The website's source code is hosted on GitHub Pages, but family records are not intentionally published in the website repository. Access to stored family records is controlled by Firebase authentication and database security rules.

Security and retention

Family data is intended to be accessible only through the authorized family Google account. Google and Firebase provide the authentication and database infrastructure. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Chore and reward records may be kept to allow the family to review previous months. The family can remove stored records or discontinue use of the dashboard. Browser-stored data can be cleared through browser settings.

Your choices

You can stop the dashboard from accessing Google Calendar by revoking the app's access in your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access prevents future Calendar retrieval unless you authorize it again. For questions or requests to correct or delete family dashboard records, contact the household administrator using the support email shown on the Google consent screen.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the dashboard's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
